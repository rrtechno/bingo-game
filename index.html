How to play

  1. Pick a game, then set the grid size, winning pattern, and call speed.
  2. Generate cards — each one gets its own card number.
  3. Print the cards and hand one to each guest (or team, for the little ones).
  4. Start the game. The tablet calls out items one at a time.
  5. Guests mark their card as items get called. When someone shouts "Bingo!", enter their card number on the tablet to confirm it.
  6. A card wins on any row, column, or diagonal — or a full black-out — depending on what the host chose at setup.
